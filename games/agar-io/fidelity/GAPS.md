## Ranked remaining limits

1. **Stochastic outline and edge pixels.** The independent bounded contour recreates small wobble and contact dents, including the previously missing growth shoulder. It does not reproduce every random original vertex excursion. The measured maximum local contour discrepancy is below2pixels in the shipped pairs; that does not establish a global bound for every crowded contact. Outline noise has no role in authoritative collisions. Canvas/SVG edge rasterization also differs at individual pixels.
2. **Font availability and rasterization.** Both renderers request Ubuntu, with native serif fallback in this font-free environment. Original text is cached at original-world resolution then scaled; SVG text uses native vector hinting, leaving small glyph-edge differences despite matching size and baseline. The screenshots compare the fallback; they do not prove the appearance of an installed original Ubuntu font. No original font is imported.
3. **Current-original access.** Live agar.io returned CloudFront403; its readable current wrapper delegates rendering to opaque WASM. This historical original reference does not establish exact2026 dynamics or audio. Current publisher mobile images independently corroborate inside names, HUD placement and circular floating controls.

Party plumbing is explicit in the game spec: shared TV overview and shell identity, phone controls, three90-second worlds, mass-at-horn totals, fair same-world returns, protection/split tuning, result summaries and reading holds. HUD and actual controls are checked separately in the mounted-controller captures. No reference skin, logo, sound or other ripped asset is used at runtime.
