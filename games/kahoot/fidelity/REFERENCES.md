The [current official guide](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408679135891-Team-experience-How-to-play-kahoot-in-groups) distinguishes shared-device and personal-device teams. It documents 5-20 seconds of talk for configurable personal-device teams, team averages, and automatic rebalancing on departures. Our fixture's long-name reading hold, fixed team membership and personal contribution receipt must be assessed explicitly against that reference; they are not evidence of near-identical behavior.

The [2021 product announcement](https://kahoot.com/blog/2021/09/22/kahoots-new-team-mode/) separately establishes individual devices contributing to average team performance. The [2019 official guide](https://kahoot.com/files/2019/08/Unconference-Takeaway-PDF-August-2019.pdf) documents the older five-second discussion beat; its image establishes host HUD placement, not current service latency or 2026 visuals.

Access limits: the official Vimeo tutorial linked from the support article was accessible as a page, but its configuration endpoint returned 403 to a desktop HTTP request. A generic question illustration endpoint also returned a Cloudflare challenge. No browser or headless process was launched. No current original play-through, animation cadence, music timing or sound fidelity is claimed. The original answer-window question screen and live final podium remain uncaptured; the Team Talk frame contains the host question layout but is not an open-answer moment.
