Geometry, HUD, motion, and terminal payoff repairs are supported by fresh matched captures, transient evidence, manual review, and the final game-only checks. The remaining font fallback is documented below.

The reference is the [original classic release at `478b6ec`](https://github.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/tree/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc). Only comparison screenshots and capture metadata are included here; original source and font assets are not included.

Ranked difference and closure record:

1. **Remaining cosmetic font difference — documented.** Original captures use Clear Sans; the port uses system Arial, an explicit fallback in the [original font stack](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc/style/main.css). Tile and HUD numbers use the original size and 700 weight, but glyph widths and shapes differ. The repository prohibits ripped font assets. This bounded typography difference remains visible and is not described as pixel-identical rendering.

2. **Board geometry and HUD — closed.** The matched captures show the 280 px mobile square, 10 px inset/gaps, small board/tile corners, title at left, and compact SCORE/BEST boxes at right. BEST is the personal stored merge-score record, separately from Best tile. The 55%/42% goal/action arrangement is restored, with the race clock in the former action slot. Absolute placement follows the host shell and race band described above. Primary reference: [original mobile CSS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc/style/main.css).

3. **Motion and score feedback — closed within the recorded method.** [`motion.json`](motion.json) reports PASS with stable source hashes for two actual reducer/controller-input scenarios and five transient frames. It records 100 ms travel, 200 ms spawn/merge effects delayed 100 ms, both merging sources, and the 600 ms rising score addition. `ours-merge-50ms.png` and `ours-merge-200ms.png` visibly show travel followed by merge/spawn. These port-only frames seek the public CSS clocks while cleanup is held; the compositor runs normally. Original timing is checked against pinned source, rather than claimed as a simultaneous original motion capture. Primary references: [CSS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc/style/main.css), [actuator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc/js/html_actuator.js).

4. **Target and last-blocked payoff — closed.** Both finishes preserve the private board for 3,000 ms: the original 1,200 ms delay and 800 ms fade plus one fully visible second. `ours-target-2500ms.png` shows the completed board and fully visible win message. After 3,200 ms of actual elapsed time, the recorded controller has no board or direction buttons and has two final ranking rows. A blocked board also remains while rivals continue. Public `terminalPayoffUntil` holds during pause and shifts on resume, preserving the exact remaining payoff after reload; both reasons add it before the full ranking reading floor. The final 119-test baseline covers genuine last-blocked transitions, target finishes, pause, and reload. Original timing: [CSS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrielecirulli/2048/478b6ec346e3787f589e4af751378d06ded4cbbc/style/main.css).

5. **Shared TV shape and fit — closed.** Fresh eight-player captures show square maps with proportional gaps/corners/numbers alongside readable score/best summaries. The independent client review manually inspected all 29 current static frames and the historical before frame: no outside-frame content, unintended clipping, board overlap, or header overlap remained. Essential TV text is at least 32 px, and all 28 measured touch targets are at least 44 px. The raw overflow count includes intentional ellipses and font ink, rather than 480 player-visible defects. Shared final standings summarize rank/name/score/best without repeating all tiles; this is party plumbing around the private original play surface. Evidence: [layout.json](../../../jobs/P09-2048-race/media/layout.json), [manual-review.json](../../../jobs/P09-2048-race/media/manual-review.json).
