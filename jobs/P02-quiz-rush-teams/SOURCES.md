# P02 sources

Mechanics use paired, independent references; content facts have their own
question-ID pairs in CONTENT_SOURCES.md. Existing P01 research is retained.

- Team discussion/captain/averaging: [official team guide](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408679135891-Team-experience-How-to-play-kahoot-in-groups), [teacher firsthand account](https://usingeducationaltechnology.com/new-team-feature-kahoot/), [independent scoring guide](https://onlineexammaker.com/kb/how-does-the-kahoot-points-system-work/), [independent team training](https://www.beesoft.com.tw/post/kahoot-team).
- TV/phone displays: [official display guide](https://kahoot.com/blog/2022/08/08/tech-tip-single-screen/), [physics teachers' guide, pp.1–4](https://www.csaapt.org/uploads/Spring2021/Guide%20to%20Kahoot.pdf).
- Speed scoring/current absence of streak points: [official points](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002303908-How-points-work), [2022 empirical study p.109](https://eurjanat.com/download/1970/), [independent scoring guide](https://onlineexammaker.com/kb/how-does-the-kahoot-points-system-work/). The paper's historical streaks differ from current policy.
- Unscored native polls: [official question types](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002308428-Kahoot-question-types?page=1), [Amnesty's authored guide p.5](https://www.amnesty.org/ru/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/POL3280472024ENGLISH.pdf).
- Offline authoring: [official spreadsheet import](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002812547-How-to-import-questions-from-a-spreadsheet-to-your-kahoot), [independent teacher guide p.9](https://www.csaapt.org/uploads/Spring2021/Guide%20to%20Kahoot.pdf).

- Static geometry/provenance: [official 2019 Team Talk PDF, p.4](https://kahoot.com/files/2019/08/Unconference-Takeaway-PDF-August-2019.pdf), [official 2021 team scoreboard announcement](https://kahoot.com/blog/2021/09/22/kahoots-new-team-mode/) and [official hosting tips, Answer results](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039900153-Tips-for-hosting-a-live-game), with the complete [2876×1576 attachment](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/article_attachments/38926500115219). The latter is a general host screenshot with two correct alternatives, not an observed team playthrough.
