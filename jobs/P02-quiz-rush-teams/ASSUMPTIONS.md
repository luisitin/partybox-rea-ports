- Resume 2026-10-12: public P01 head 4043226 and green CI resolve the prerequisite blocker. P02 extends that canonical module directly. Wager remains the default, Classic remains opt-in, and groups preserve the canonical 300-point streak cap.
- Read-only core inspection found T-1146 already implements wager teams but excludes discussion, room vote and donor packs. UPGRADE.md records the future owner-adapter scope; this job changes no private checkout or deployed host.
- The current manifest inherits the canonical 26-minute estimate. Fresh group simulations must establish the bound; older 15-minute evidence does not release this integration.
- The owner authorizes this public clone, main CLAIMS commits, gh PRs and continued queue work. These session instructions override the internal team-board process. Each claim commit follows pull --rebase. No board or other runner checkout is modified.
- Owner 10-11 requires the public workshop id/name kahoot/Kahoot!. Existing job and branch paths remain stable. Owner-core adaptation retains the existing Lightning Round card and registration.
- Teams use personal devices and seeded fixed balanced membership. Eligible points are averaged per question; disconnected unanswered members leave the denominator while accepted departing answers remain. This local rule protects teammates from absent seats and raw-sum size bias.
- Room vote is one cooperative group. Accepted phone votes elect a plurality answer, seeded randomness breaks ties, and mean vote time determines shared credit. This is an original adaptation of native unscored polls.
- Discussion defaults to five seconds and respects the SDK reading floor. Speed timing starts when the answer window opens; discussion and pauses do not consume that window.
- Original interactive team play was not performed without a live room PIN or captain account. Paired published guides, screenshots and firsthand reports reconstruct the original flow.
- Owner files compile to content/classic-custom.json before bundling. Custom packs have 5–20 questions with explicit custom-prefixed IDs, and require a host rebuild/restart. The reducer performs no runtime filesystem/network calls or content injection. Unknown custom facts use seeded guesses rather than hidden keys.
