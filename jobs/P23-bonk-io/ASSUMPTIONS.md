# P23 Bonk.io assumptions and limitations

Recorded 2026-10-12 UTC by r4-local. Update implementation choices here as tuning changes.

1. **Official name.** JOBS.md's owner naming table overrides the older working-title wording. Display name is Bonk.io; folder/manifest id is bonk-io. Original art and no copied assets remain required.
2. **No original live play.** The parent inspected computer-use surfaces: no apps/browsers were available; selecting bonk.io returned `No browser is available`. We used the official site/client and independent written sources. No claim is made of original live gameplay, heard sound or measured video timings. This limitation alone does not block the job under RULES.md's fallback instructions.
3. **Source independence.** Official pages/client resources are one source family; all community wiki articles are one family; the university lab/catalog are one family. The separate game guide corroborates qualitative controls, with unknown editorial borrowing. It does not establish exact physics values.
4. **Public client reading.** HTTP text inspection was read-only and in memory. The obfuscated client was not decoded or executed; no originals were saved into the worktree. Resource labels establish sound availability only. Temporary HTTP text is not shipped source or media.
5. **Authored motion.** Gravity, movement acceleration, drag, maximum speed, ball radius, bounce, collision impulse, solver iterations, timestep and grounded jump strength/holding behavior are authored. The university lab is an explanatory clone, not a source for matching original coefficients. Bonk.io's original downward steering/air-control details are not fully measured; P23 specifically asks for left/right/jump plus heavy.
6. **Authored heavy behavior.** Stronger impact/resistance with reduced mobility is corroborated. The wiki's exact mass increase and fatigue/recovery curve were not independently measured in the current original. Any fixed multiplier, depletion or recharge values in PartyBox must be specified as authored and checked for usefulness/fairness.
7. **Authored match pacing/scoring.** Countdown existence, rounds-to-win controls and last-standing outcomes are corroborated; original elapsed countdown/reveal durations, defaults, timeout rules, simultaneous falls, draws, elimination points and overall ties are unknown. Finite round count/caps, sudden death, shared ties and seat rotation are adaptation rules, not claims about the original. README/tests must match the final choices.
8. **Authored audio/art.** No music or recording is reused. Existing SDK feedback cues can mark collision/elimination/reveal beats; exact original timbre, mix and playback rate are unknown. SVG/CSS balls, arena geometry and numbered identity marks are original. No logo, skin or community map is copied.
9. **Presence.** PartyBox needs temporary reconnect to preserve a ball, disconnect to release held controls, permanent leave/kick to retire the player, and spectators/eliminated phones to keep the public stage. These are PartyBox contract decisions rather than measured Bonk.io network behavior. Stable slots remain identifiable after departures.
10. **Overlap.** The owner's PartyBox checkout was read only. Actual manifests and generated registry both contain 110 games with no Bonk entry; the human catalog count is stale. The closest ball/jump/survival games inspected have different rules, so this job adds bonk-io rather than upgrading one of them.
11. **Extracted-repo commands.** `games/AGENTS.md` references `docs/ADDING_A_GAME.md`, `pnpm gen-registry` and `pnpm verify`, but this public extraction has no such document or scripts. Available scripts are typecheck/format and Vitest. Follow RULES.md's real available checks and the owner's own-game test scope; do not substitute a whole-suite run or edit the owner monorepo. Root owns verification, CI, claims, pushes and PR creation.
12. **CPU/browser scope.** Subagents run no browser or heavy simulation. Only the owning runner may launch the one permitted headless browser, with cleanup in a finally block. Tests remain scoped to games/bonk-io; no whole-suite browser run.
13. **Above-arena stress pose.** The bounded public model permits live y positions above the SVG; the fall rule has no upper kill plane. Numbered upward markers preserve identity for those legal poses. The two above-arena captures are explicitly authored stress states (-80/-10 y), not claimed original live observations or naturally sampled bot positions. Full simulations record their minimum alive y separately.
14. **Connection feedback.** The public extraction exposes the own player's connected flag, but no local SDK connection hook or room shell/remount implementation. The controller resets on that flag and browser offline/online events; SSR/session regressions and two reducer-generated disconnect captures cover the observable view/session paths. The online reset also clears attempts made during a browser outage. No live WebSocket/remount integration result is claimed.
