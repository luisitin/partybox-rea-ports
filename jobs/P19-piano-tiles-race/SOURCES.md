## Assets

The game uses original CSS tile/track art, system fonts and existing PartyBox design tokens and sound cues. There are no downloaded images, song recordings, sprites, fonts, logos or third-party assets. No additional licence is needed beyond this repository's MIT game licence.

Screens and static render evidence are produced from this port's own fixtures; their hashes are recorded separately in SHA256SUMS.txt.
