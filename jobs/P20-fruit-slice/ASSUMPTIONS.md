# P20 Fruit Slice assumptions

- The explicit REA-runner request governs this clone, its claim/main commits and
  GitHub PR workflow. The PartyBox team manual describes a different internal
  checkout/board workflow; no board or owner checkout is touched.
- P20's 60-second brief uses the reference's Arcade-style score attack. Classic
  bomb elimination and three-miss game-over would conflict with a room in which
  every player stays active for the full minute.
- A bomb resets a streak, as the job explicitly specifies. Any additional score
  penalty, streak thresholds and score multipliers are port design decisions and
  must be documented in the final spec and scoring tests.
- No hands-on original-game play occurred in this research subtask. Native access
  was not tested by installing/purchasing a game. Public original guidance and an
  independently authored guide/manual provide the corroborated mechanics.
- Original fruit trajectories, gesture tolerances, exact launch cadence, Blitz
  timeout and audio timing were not measured. Implementation constants will be
  authored and verified as PartyBox choices.
- The reachable playfruitninja.com public bundle has inconsistent naming and no
  established Halfbrick provenance. It was inspected as text only and supplies
  no authority for original mechanics or asset permission.
- An unofficial reverse-engineered Bada client was inspected only as a research
  lead. Its acknowledged TODO/DIFFERS comments prevent treating it as ground
  truth. No code, binaries, images or audio is copied.
- Research fetch failures do not block the job: official pages, a licensed
  cabinet manual, independent coverage and an independent guide were reachable.
- Identical seeded opportunities per player are recommended for fair 2-8-player
  competition. The TV can show one shared fountain while the phone owns that
  player's accepted slices and meter. Disconnects cannot pause other players.
- No test, browser, Git command or file outside these three research documents
  was changed by this research subtask. Root owns integration and verification.
