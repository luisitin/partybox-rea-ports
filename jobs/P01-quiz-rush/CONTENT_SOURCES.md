# Quiz Rush content fact audit

Checked 2026-10-11. Every prompt, distractor and explanation was written for this port; no trivia question text, artwork or other assets were copied. The links below verify the underlying facts, with two independently published references for each question or fact family. These are studio/distributor, film-institute, government, museum, scientific-institution and sport-governing-body pages; none is a trivia-question collection.

Difficulty is an editorial estimate (1 = accessible, 2 = some recall, 3 = specialist recall), not a claim measured by these sources. Numeric, geographic and film facts deliberately avoid current officeholders, live records, changing populations and sequel-dependent wording.

## Audit decisions

- Eight-planet questions explicitly use the recognized planet set, preventing Pluto from becoming an ambiguous alternative.
- Everest specifies height above sea level, rather than base-to-summit height.
- Basketball and volleyball specify their standard indoor/full-court forms; reduced-player variants do not change the answer.
- Inside Out questions specify the original film, because its sequel adds emotions.
- A standard day means the conventional time unit, rather than Earth's sidereal rotation.
- Correct answer positions are evenly distributed in the authored packs and are shuffled by the server.
- Bot knowledge records answer text by question ID; it contains no option index and can be matched after option shuffling.
- Structural content review found exactly 20 questions per pack, 60 unique IDs, exactly four options per question, no duplicate options, prompts at most 86 characters, options at most 17 characters and explanations at most 67 characters.
- Schema permits 1–200 questions so the P02 editor can add shorter or longer original packs. It rejects duplicate IDs, duplicate answer text ignoring case, extra fields and out-of-range correct indexes.
- A second content review clarified association football as soccer and removed Carl's name from the balloon question so it does not reveal the answer to the separate character-name question. All 60 keys, explanations, unique options and bot answer facts were checked again; every pack still has 20 questions.
