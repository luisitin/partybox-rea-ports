# Quiz Rush content fact audit

Checked 2026-10-11. Every prompt, distractor and explanation was written for this port; no trivia question text, artwork or other assets were copied. The links below verify the underlying facts, with two independently published references for each question or fact family. These are studio/distributor, film-institute, government, museum, scientific-institution, orchestra and sport-governing-body pages; none is a trivia-question collection.

Difficulty is an editorial estimate (1 = accessible, 2 = some recall, 3 = specialist recall), not a claim measured by these sources. Numeric, geographic and film facts deliberately avoid current officeholders, live records, changing populations and sequel-dependent wording.

## Audit decisions

- Planet questions use the recognized planet set; none relies on treating Pluto as a planet.
- Everest specifies height above sea level, rather than base-to-summit height.
- Basketball and volleyball specify their standard indoor/full-court forms; reduced-player variants do not change the answer.
- Inside Out questions specify the original film, because its sequel adds emotions.
- A standard day means the conventional time unit, rather than Earth's sidereal rotation.
- Correct answer positions are evenly distributed in the authored packs and are shuffled by the server.
- Bot knowledge records answer text by question ID; it contains no option index and can be matched after option shuffling.
- Structural content review found exactly 20 questions per pack, 60 unique IDs, exactly four options per question, no duplicate options, prompts at most 86 characters, options at most 23 characters and explanations at most 67 characters.
- Schema permits 1–200 questions so the P02 editor can add shorter or longer original packs. It rejects duplicate IDs, duplicate answer text ignoring case, extra fields and out-of-range correct indexes.
- A second content review clarified association football as soccer and removed Carl's name from the balloon question so it does not reveal the answer to the separate character-name question. All 60 keys, explanations, unique options and bot answer facts were checked again; every pack still has 20 questions.

- KEEP GOING round 3 replaced six duplicate animated-film questions with six distinct live-action films: The Wizard of Oz (classic musical/fantasy), Home Alone (comedy), Raiders of the Lost Ark (adventure), Men in Black (sci-fi comedy), Jaws (thriller), and the first Harry Potter film (fantasy). The pack remains 20 questions, expands from 10 to 16 films, and reduces animation questions from 14 to 8. The six replacements preserve their correct indices; bot answer text and both factual references were updated together. Their prompts, options and explanations are at most 67, 17 and 67 characters respectively.

- KEEP GOING round 4 replaced science-04/05/06/10/12/20 with heart circulation, magnetic repulsion, evaporation, atmospheric nitrogen, dropped-ball gravity and basalt; general-03/04 now ask about violin strings and the Mona Lisa. Astronomy falls from 10/20 to 6/20 and general geography from 9/20 to 7/20 (capital questions from three to one). Three demonstrated answer-cue relationships are removed: science-05 gave science-06's planet count, science-12 gave science-11's water formula, and science-18/19 gave science-20's photosynthesis energy source. Whole-pack review compares prompts and explanations as well as keys; shared words with different relationships are not treated as cues. All eight original correct indices are preserved; all 60 authored answers match bot knowledge. The eight replacement prompts, options and explanations are at most 69, 23 and 65 characters respectively. Each replacement's two independently published institutional sources was opened and checked against the specific fact, including the explanation.
