# P10 — Assumptions

- The owner's explicit REA runner instructions define this work in `C:/dev/rea-runs/r9`, identity `r9-local`, CLAIMS.md on this public repository's main, per-job branches and GitHub PRs. They take precedence over generic PartyBox `pb` board/worktree, model, submission, no-main-push and task-selection instructions read under `C:/dev`. No PartyBox board or other worktree is changed.
- P10's three rounds are fixed. A round clock was not specified; use 120 s by default with a `roundSeconds` setting from 60 to 180 in 15 s steps. A 9 s reveal lets eight strangers read the answer and points. These are local pacing decisions, not measured original timings.
- Round ranking is fewest accepted guesses, then active elapsed solve time. One point per opponent beaten converts rank to finite match points. Every unsolved player ties and earns zero; exact solve ties earn equal points. Match score is the sum of three rounds.
- The licensed product PDF, original tutorial project and independent clone are sufficient fallback mechanics evidence under RULES.md. Official NYT web fetches failed; CUA original navigation timed out, its clone retry reported unavailable `iab`, and the inventory contained no apps or browsers. No live source play or original audio/animation timing is claimed. Source implementation was read for semantics only.
- The shipped house dictionary is intentionally bounded: 260 common answers and 1264 accepted words from independent editorial selection. It is not the original's dictionary and not every possible English word. Answers and guesses accept case normalization; punctuation, spaces inside a word, numerals, accented characters and any length other than five ASCII letters are invalid.
- Sending the same normalized word again is treated as an accidental duplicate and costs no attempt. This is a fairness/usability choice; no claim is made that the original rejects repeated guesses.
- A submit waits at most three seconds for acknowledgement, then keeps the draft and offers retry. Paused or disconnected inputs can be dropped by the shell; server duplicate rejection makes retry safe even when the first acknowledgement was lost. Editing a rejected draft clears its old feedback.
- Reveal times display milliseconds, matching the speed comparison, and round winners are called out separately from cumulative match standings.
- Public TV progress always omits letters, also after a player's individual solve or exhaustion. Only that player's own accepted words are returned privately; the shared answer appears only after the round exits. The phone cannot reveal it to an exhausted player before the rest of the room.
- No connected players is not an all-finished early-exit case. The phase deadline remains armed; permanently left and disconnected seats retain their prior scores in final results.
- Bot strengths operate only on the curated public answer universe and their own clue history, using supplied seeded RNG. Three strengths refer to easy, normal and sharp, following the SDK's skill names.
- JOBS.md requests the reference manifest's tags, so the port keeps `["comedy"]` alongside its required `arcade` category.
- CPU care from the owner wins over broader reference commands: only this game's Vitest path, no whole-suite browser run, and at most one headless browser managed by the root runner with cleanup in `finally`.
