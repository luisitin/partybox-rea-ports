## Port content and assets

- `games/word-guess-race/content/words.json`: original editorial selections of everyday five-letter English words, selected independently for this job; not copied from a source word list. 260 answer entries, 1204 accepted entries, unique within each list. The accepted list contains the entire answer list. Common single words may naturally overlap other dictionaries.
- The TV and phone artwork consists of our CSS tile grid and standard check/arrow/cross glyphs, styled with published PartyBox tokens. No brand or source-game logo is used.
- Audio uses PartyBox SDK sound cues only. No recording, music or source audio was downloaded.
- All runtime data ships locally; there are no remote dictionaries, trackers or runtime network requests.
- Original code and editorial content are licensed under this repository's MIT licence. SDK primitives and design tokens retain the repository licence. No third-party asset licences are needed.
