## Asset licences

- Block Clash's grid, blocks, ghost, arrows and feedback are original SVG/CSS authored for this repository and released with the game under the repository's MIT licence.
- Colors, typography and motion use the existing PartyBox design tokens and SDK.
- No third-party bitmap, font, logo, music or sound file is used. No asset download is needed at runtime.
- No runtime network access, analytics or tracking is introduced.

One additional manufacturer manual (`I1045.pdf`, Challenge) was found through indexed text; direct PDF opening timed out. The accessible `I4063.pdf` and Xbox manual cover the relevant versus facts, so the inaccessible document is not needed as evidence.
