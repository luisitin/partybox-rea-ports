## Claim-to-source rule

Broad gameplay claims in RESEARCH.md use two independent sources. Where the available evidence does not establish exact current behavior (head-head arbitration, mass drain, minimum, corpse yield, sound timing), RESEARCH.md explicitly labels uncertainty and the chosen PartyBox adaptation. Numerical tuning belongs in the game specification and ASSUMPTIONS.md, not in a claim of exact original fidelity.

No copied artwork, sound effects, music, logos, fonts, or code. No sources are loaded at runtime.
